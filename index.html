How should the day feel?

Every fragrance is named for the number of raw materials in it, blended the week of your wedding, and labelled with your names. Choose a feeling and watch the room change.

Naturally derived

Essential oils and natural isolates. No synthetic musks, no phthalates, no aerosols.

Pet-safe formulations

Blended and diffused at levels safe around dogs and cats. The venue's resident lurcher is not an afterthought.

Dial it up or down

Venue-safe by design

No naked flame, no spray, no residue. Works in listed buildings and barns where candles are banned.

01 / The Eight

Named for what's in them

Chapel holds seven raw materials. Ember holds thirty-one. The number is the formula, not a marketing device. Open any of them to read what's inside and decide how far through the day it runs.

02 / Label

Your names on every bottle

Every bottle, favour, diffuser and invitation carries the same label: the fragrance, your names, the place, the date. It's printed the week the batch is blended, which is also the week of your wedding.

A guest who takes a favour home is keeping your names on a shelf for years. That is the whole argument for fragrance at a wedding, so the label belongs at the centre rather than in the corner.

Leave it blank and the label reads "personalisation: you"
