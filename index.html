01 / What We Do

What we do

You choose one of eight fragrances, and you choose how far through the day it runs. We blend it the week of the wedding, put your names on every label, and post it to you. There is no boutique and no appointment.

01Choose a fragrance Eight of them, each named for the number of raw materials in it. Free samples in the post first, if you would rather smell before deciding.
02Choose how far it runs Three tiers. The formula is identical in all three — only its reach changes, from the ceremony room to the favours to the invitations.
03We blend it that week Batched the week of the wedding and labelled with your names, the place and the date. Nothing sits on a shelf for a year first.
04It arrives ready to use Diffusion, room mists and a placement plan drawn up for your venue. No naked flame, no spray, no residue.

It works the same for a birthday, an anniversary or a christening. A wedding is simply where it gets used most.

02 / The Eight

Named for what's in them

Chapel holds seven raw materials. Ember holds thirty-one. The number is the formula, not a marketing device. Open any of them to read what's inside and decide how far through the day it runs.

03 / Label

Your names on every bottle

Every bottle, favour, diffuser and invitation carries the same label: the fragrance, your names, the place, the date. It's printed the week the batch is blended, which is also the week of your wedding.

A guest who takes a favour home is keeping your names on a shelf for years. That is the whole argument for fragrance at a wedding, so the label belongs at the centre rather than in the corner.

Leave it blank and the label reads "personalisation: you"
